Alias Name: Terry
Age: Unknown
Gender: Male
Specimen: Pterodactyl
Weight: 87 pounds
Height (Wingspan): ~7 ft
Mutation Classification: Level 4

Description:
Incident C-202512, referred to as Terry, is a large, sharp-eyed pterodactyl often utilized for aerial tasks and fast transport. Among the few flying mutations that are actually controllable to some degree, Terry is known for his incredible mobility in the air, using his large wingspan to navigate harsh skies and tight spaces with ease.

Terry isn’t necessarily aggressive, but his size and flight speed make him a force to be cautious around. He’s not trained for combat, but his sharp beak and claws can cause damage if provoked or startled. Fortunately, Terry tends to stay calm when he's flying—it’s when he's grounded that he becomes more restless and unpredictable.

His usefulness comes mainly from scouting, transporting, and quick aerial evacuations. While not completely obedient, he follows commands well enough if handled by someone familiar. His intelligence seems limited compared to other incidents, but his instincts in the air are unmatched.
