Description:
Incident P-8914, Also referred to as Dolphin, is a sleek, intelligent aquatic creature commonly spotted around the beach zones near the facility. Unlike more aggressive marine incidents, Dolphin is non-hostile and appears to enjoy the company of others, frequently seen swimming alongside Incident H-1125 (Whale) and the nearby shark units stationed in the ocean perimeter.

Its primary trait is exceptional swimming speed, able to navigate ocean currents faster than most aquatic incidents. While it has no known offensive abilities or mutations beyond enhanced agility, its speed makes it an excellent scout or courier for small items between areas—if it chooses to cooperate.

Despite being a low-level incident, Dolphin shows signs of curiosity and higher-level awareness. It is considered safe for observation and probably one of the chillest/laid back creatures to be around with.
