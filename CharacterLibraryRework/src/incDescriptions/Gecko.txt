Description:
Incident E-31115, also referred to as Incident Gecko, is a sleek, nimble reptilian entity known for its unmatched reaction speed. It is nearly impossible to land a hit on, no matter the method or angle—physical attacks, projectiles, and even advanced targeting systems have all failed. By the time a strike is made, Gecko has already repositioned, often unpredictably.

Though it lacks offensive capabilities or elemental powers, its speed alone makes it an anomaly worth studying, especially in high-risk evasion scenarios. It has shown no signs of aggression and avoids confrontation whenever possible, slipping away with ease before things escalate.

Due to its elusive behavior and lack of hostility, containment is minimal, requiring only motion-triggered perimeter systems to log movement data. The entity remains unnamed, though researchers have noted its almost playful tendency to dodge anything sent its way.
