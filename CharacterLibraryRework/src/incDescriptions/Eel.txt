Alias Name: Eeldrin
Age: Unknown
Gender: Male
Specimen: Eel
Weight: 72 pounds
Height: 6 ft 9 in
Mutation Classification: Level 3

Description:
Incident E-9118, and more casually known by his alias Eeldrin, is one of the more smug-looking creatures within containment. With a constant confident smirk and a slithery calm demeanor, Eeldrin almost appears proud of what he does—and what he does is shock people, quite literally.

Eeldrin uses his long, flexible tail to wrap around his target in a tight coil. Once his grip is locked in and the victim can’t escape, he delivers a burst of electricity—not fatal, but definitely enough to leave someone stunned, twitching, and full of regret. While not considered lethal, repeated shocks can temporarily paralyze muscles or disorient the victim.

Despite his cocky presence, Eeldrin doesn’t go looking for trouble unless provoked. However, he enjoys testing boundaries, and has been known to sneak up on individuals simply to assert dominance with a playful jolt. It’s not uncommon to hear his containment handlers refer to him as a “walking taser with attitude.”
