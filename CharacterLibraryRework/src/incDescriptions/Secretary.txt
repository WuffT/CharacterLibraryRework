Description:
At first glance, Incident T-11825 appears to be nothing more than a well-dressed, tall-legged bird with an intense stare. However, this entity possesses an unusual ability—its voice. When it speaks, subjects have reported an overwhelming sense of trust and calm, often becoming agreeable to whatever is being said. This persuasive tone has been dubbed as "verbal hypnosis" by some staff, though it is not mind control in the traditional sense.

Incident Secretary Bird is non-hostile and quite polite, but containment procedures recommend limiting verbal interaction unless necessary. Prolonged conversation has led to staff following absurd instructions without realizing they were even given.
