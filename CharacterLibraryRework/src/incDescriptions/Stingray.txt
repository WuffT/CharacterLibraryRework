Description:
Designed to work in sync with Incident G-12518 (Angler) and Incident I-4518 (Spider), Incident Stingray is one of the more tactical creatures under Stenfort’s creation log. Despite being the smallest incident so far—standing at only 3 feet tall—this slippery unit makes up for its size with utility.

Its core ability is a quick, paralyzing static shock that temporarily disables motor function. It's completely painless—those affected describe it as the strange “static” feeling you get when a limb falls asleep. While the effect doesn’t last long, it’s more than enough time for other incidents like the Spider or Angler to move in.

Due to its light weight and silent movement, Stingray is difficult to detect, making it a perfect support unit. It doesn’t often engage on its own, preferring to lurk until backup arrives.

Handle with care, and don’t underestimate it just because it’s small.
