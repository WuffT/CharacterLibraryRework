Incident P-201518, designated Incident Raptor, is a mutated orange/blue reptilian specimen exhibiting extreme temperature modulation abilities. It can raise or lower its body temperature drastically based on its emotional state or surrounding environment, shifting between intense heat or chilling cold with little warning. 

Comparable in speed to (Farlow) or (Carter), Flaire sacrifices flight or gliding capabilities for greater physical resilience. Its body is well-adapted to harsh conditions, and it shows minimal damage from blunt force or temperature extremes. Despite its reptilian appearance, it demonstrates behavior patterns more closely aligned with mood-driven entities, making it highly unpredictable in volatile settings.

Because of the tight link between its emotional state and its temperature-altering mutation, standard control measures are often ineffective. Personnel are advised to maintain calm and avoid making direct contact during observation. Sudden movements, loud noises, or changes in environmental temperature may provoke an unwanted response.
