Description:
Incident C-120, often just referred to as “the Cat,” is an unusual feline-based specimen most known for its anomalous ability to possess exactly nine lives. These lives appear to reset weekly, regardless of how many were used during that time. This regeneration is not just metaphorical—multiple fatal scenarios have been observed where the incident simply revives moments later, entirely unharmed.

The Cat has been seen walking away from explosions, surviving direct hits, and even leaping from impossible heights with no visible injury. It does so with an unsettling nonchalance and an eerie awareness of its own invulnerability, at least while its lives are active.

Though not particularly hostile, the Cat is extremely elusive, playful, and at times a nuisance—often using its immortality to cheat facility protocols and containment procedures. Once all nine lives are used, it becomes fully vulnerable until the reset occurs, making that window of time the only safe opportunity for direct interaction or procedure.

A living paradox—death is temporary, mischief is permanent.
