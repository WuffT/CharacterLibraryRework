Alias Name: Gavrin
Age: Unknown
Gender: Male
Specimen: Worm
Weight: 146 pounds
Height: 6 ft
Mutation Classification: Level 4

Description:
Gavrin, known officially as Incident W-151813, is a towering worm-like entity with a volatile emotional aura. Specifically, it emits a rage-inducing presence that can escalate tempers and cause aggressive behavior in any nearby individuals—human or creature alike. This effect grows stronger the longer one remains in proximity and has proven difficult to counter in solo containment scenarios.

Fortunately, Gavrin is rarely found alone. It is frequently accompanied by N-1115 (Nixan), the joy-spreading purple snake, and A-71514 (Sombryl), the sorrowful dragon. The unique emotional balance between these three incidents appears to counteract and stabilize each other’s influences. Together, they create a neutral emotional zone—one of the rare few where Gavrin’s presence does not result in outbreaks of hostility.

Despite its fearsome emotional ability, Gavrin is not inherently violent. Its behavior is mostly passive when not agitated and has shown signs of intelligence and awareness of its effect on others.

One entity, many tempers—contained only through companionship.
