Age: Unknown
Gender: Male
Specimen: T-Rex-like Creature
Weight: 155 pounds
Height: 5 ft 5 in
Mutation Classification: Level 5

Description:
Incident T-18524 (code-named “We Don’t See The Light”) is a strange, shadowy creature that closely resembles some kind of T-Rex. What’s odd about it is that it seems to induce fear in people just by being nearby. It doesn’t charge in or make loud noises—it prefers to stalk. It usually picks one person at a time, quietly watching from a distance, hidden in the dark. Most don’t even realize it’s there until they catch a glimpse of something in their peripheral vision… and by the time they turn to look, it’s already gone.

One of the most disturbing encounters involving Tyerux happened during a time that resulted in N-1115 (Nixan) being dragged into what appeared to be a different realm entirely. Though Nixan eventually returned unharmed, he was clearly shaken. Based on what he described, Tyerux doesn't just stalk people physically—it studies them. It waits, watches, and learns how they think. It’s not interested in attacking the body—it wants to get inside your mind. It feeds off fear, anxiety, hesitation… anything that suggests you're scared.
