Incident I-14715 (Flamingo)

This is the second known file of a Level 5 incident and notably the first Level 5 that has not been immediately terminated upon its creation. Unlike the previous Level 5 incident, This Flamingo is non-aggressive and demonstrates a calm, creative nature (Quite literally).

It possesses an extraordinary ability to bring its paintings to life. Anything it paints on a canvas—such as an apple, a brick, or a ball—materializes in the real world as a physical object. However, this ability has its limits as they cannot create abstract concepts like “god” or microscopic entities like a “single atom” or “cell.” Despite its immense power to draw almost anything at will and create it, It chooses not to resort to violence or aggression.
