Description:
Incident O-2312, a mysterious forest-dwelling owl-like creature, is known for its deep connection to nature—particularly to forested areas. What makes this entity so unique is its unusual and still poorly understood ability to grow massively in size, far beyond natural limits. Documented sightings suggest it can grow up to three times the height of a standard tree, though the actual upper limit remains undetermined.

Despite its towering and imposing form during these transformations, O-2312 is not considered hostile by default. In fact, the entity has shown signs of peaceful, guardian-like behavior, often seen watching over large forested sections as if acting as a sentinel or protector.

Whether Dr. Stenfort created it to protect nature or it simply chose to take on that role is still unknown, but its presence is consistently associated with peace, quiet, and balance. Often referred to as Nature’s Wind, this entity acts more as a guardian than a mere anomaly—watching over the forest with a silent, calming authority.
