Description:
Incident S-12217, better known as Incident Slug, is a slow-moving, sticky organism that shares a physical texture and consistency similar to that of an axolotl. Despite its sluggish appearance, it holds an unusual charm—particularly through its love for music.

The Slug is known for emitting a soft, musical hum that has a calming, almost hypnotic effect on both human and non-human listeners. This humming ability can ease tension, soothe aggression, and in some cases, induce sleep. The sound is not constant but is triggered either by environmental music or the creature’s mood. It has shown a clear preference for being near radios, speakers, or even live instruments, often humming in harmony with the melodies it hears.

Though non-aggressive and largely passive, its sticky body makes physical interaction difficult, as anything that touches it tends to get stuck for a brief moment. However, it poses no threat and is considered harmless under all known circumstances.

Containment is low-risk, but it is encouraged to provide access to music during observation. This not only improves its mood but enhances its effectiveness in calming nearby entities.
