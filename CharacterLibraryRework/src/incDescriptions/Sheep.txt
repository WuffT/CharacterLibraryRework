Description:
Incident H-5516, referred and nicknamed Shepern, is a surprisingly protective and territorial sheep-like entity. Despite its seemingly docile appearance, Shepern exhibits strong defensive behaviors when its surroundings are threatened.

One of its most notable abilities is generating a powerful shockwave by stomping its hooves firmly on the ground. This shockwave can knock back and destabilize anything within a moderate radius. Alongside this physical force, Shepern emits a loud, resonant "Baaaa!" — a piercing vocalization that causes disorientation and confusion in nearby subjects, impairing their coordination and focus temporarily.

These abilities make Shepern effective at deterring intruders or potential threats within its containment zone, earning it a reputation as a "formidable protector despite its otherwise gentle demeanor".
