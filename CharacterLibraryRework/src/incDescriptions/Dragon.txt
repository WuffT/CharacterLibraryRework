Alias Name: Sombryl
Age: Unknown
Gender: Male
Specimen: Dragon
Weight: 110 pounds
Height: 5 ft 4 in
Mutation Classification: Level 4

Description:
Incident A-71514, also referred to as Sombryl, is a deeply troubled and sorrowful creature burdened by an unknown trauma that seems to weigh heavily on its very presence. It exudes an aura of melancholy that affects anyone nearby, causing repressed memories to surface and a profound sense of sadness to take hold. This emotional heaviness is so intense that proximity to Sombryl is often warned against for mental well-being.

In stark contrast to N-1115 (the purple snake known for spreading joy), Sombryl brings an overwhelming feeling of sorrow and repression. Despite being polar opposites in emotional influence, these two are surprisingly close friends, along with Incident W-151813 (The angry worm), forming a unique bond grounded in their shared experiences and complementary energies.
