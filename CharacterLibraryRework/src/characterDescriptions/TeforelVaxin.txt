Description:
Teforel is the composed counterpart to Zalfor Tylox’s volatility. Where Zalfor thrives on chaos and impulsive decisions, Teforel is guided by patience, logic, and quiet discipline. He serves as the stabilizing force behind Lizzord Island’s leadership, often acting not out of loyalty to Zalfor’s authority, but out of genuine concern for the island and its people.

Though rarely in the spotlight, Teforel is deeply involved in shaping the island’s strength—training the younger Lizzords with precision and intent. He values structure and growth over power, preferring strategy to spectacle. Unlike Zalfor, Teforel doesn’t crave recognition; his satisfaction comes from progress and balance.

Despite their opposing temperaments, Teforel harbors a quiet fondness for Zalfor. He doesn’t always agree with his chaotic decisions, but sees something redeemable in him—a longtime companion lost in his own frustrations. There’s a sense of reluctant loyalty, a belief that Zalfor could be more if he could only learn to slow down and listen. In that way, Teforel sticks around not just out of duty—but out of being there for his friend.
