Description:
Rexel is the leader of the shark bodyguard unit stationed at the facility, recognized for their physical strength and dedication to protective duties. As the frontline defense against intruders or unauthorized individuals, this group is trained in both non-lethal and high-force takedown methods. Despite their appearance, these workers are generally cooperative and follow commands with reliability.

Dr. Stenfort, has noted the shark units to be occasionally clumsy in execution, though consistently effective. Rexel, the light blue shark often seen at the front, maintains order among his subordinates and ensures objectives are completed with minimal error. Though not known for subtlety, Rexel and his team are incredibly valued.
