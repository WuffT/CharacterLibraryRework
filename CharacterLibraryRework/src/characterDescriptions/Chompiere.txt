Name: Chompiére Rouxhart
Age: 28
Gender: Male
Species: Crocodile (Chef-Class)
Weight: 152 pounds
Height: 6 ft 9 in
Role Classification: Facility Chef

Description:
Chompiére serves as the facility’s head chef and is widely respected among both staff and, surprisingly, certain classified entities. Formerly an underappreciated cook in a civilian kitchen, Chompiére joined the facility seeking a new purpose and has since become an integral part of daily operations. His meals have been credited with raising morale among workers and satisfying even the most peculiar nutritional demands from various incidents.

He is known for his professionalism, passion for culinary arts, and the genuine warmth he brings to every dish. Though initially overwhelmed by the facility’s strange and unpredictable nature, Chompiére has since found his place and now thrives within its unconventional structure. Even Dr. Stenfort, not one to give praise lightly, once remarked, “Your cooking reminds me of the meals my parents used to make…” which is a rare but HIGHLY praised compliment.
